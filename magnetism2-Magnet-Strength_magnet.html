Falling Magnet -- Induced EMF

A bar magnet falls through a coil -- the changing flux induces an EMF (Faraday's law)

Flux linkage NΦ(y) = N B K / (1 + y²/a²)3/2, coil radius a = 6 cm  |  Faraday: ε = −d(NΦ)/dt = −(d(NΦ)/dy)·v  |  Free fall: v = −g t, y = h − ½g t²  |  Flipping the magnet or the coil leads reverses the sign of ε  |  The areas ∫ε dt under the two peaks are equal and opposite: the flux rises, then falls back to zero
