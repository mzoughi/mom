$hintlink[10]
  1. Record three runs with the same `theta` and `d` but different masses. How does the measured acceleration depend on the mass `m`?
  2. Keeping `m` and `d` fixed, record runs at several angles. How does the acceleration change as `theta` increases?
  3. For one of your runs, compare the measured acceleration to `g sin theta` with `g = 9.80` m/s². What is the percent difference?
  4. Holding `theta` fixed, record runs at several release distances. Graph displacement vs. `T^2` and show the linear fit. How is the slope related to the acceleration?
  5. Use the release height `h` to predict the final speed from `v_f = sqrt(2gh)`. How does your prediction compare with the recorded `v_f`?
