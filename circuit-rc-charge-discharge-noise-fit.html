The SP3T switch has three positions: CHARGE (connects battery through R1 and R2 to capacitor), OPEN (no connection -- capacitor holds its charge), and DISCHARGE (capacitor discharges through R2 and R3). Set R1 or R2 to 0 to short them. Set R3 to 0 to remove the discharge branch entirely.
