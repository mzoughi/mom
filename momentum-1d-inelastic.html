This simulation shows two carts, red and blue, in a perfectly inelastic collision: they stick together and move on as one.

No outside horizontal force acts during the collision, so the total momentum is conserved:

`m_R v_(R_i) + m_B v_(B_i) = (m_R + m_B) v_f`.

Kinetic energy is not conserved; some of it becomes heat and deformation. The kinetic energy lost is

`Delta K = 1/2 m_R v_(R_i)^2 + 1/2 m_B v_(B_i)^2 - 1/2 (m_R + m_B) v_f^2`.

Change the masses and the initial velocities and run the simulation; the readout gives the momentum of each cart before and after, and the totals. This simulation is useful for studying conservation of momentum and inelastic collisions.
