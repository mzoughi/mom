This simulation shows two carts, red and blue, in an elastic collision: they bounce off each other with no loss of kinetic energy.

In an elastic collision both the total momentum and the total kinetic energy are conserved:

`m_R v_(R_i) + m_B v_(B_i) = m_R v_(R_f) + m_B v_(B_f)`,

`1/2 m_R v_(R_i)^2 + 1/2 m_B v_(B_i)^2 = 1/2 m_R v_(R_f)^2 + 1/2 m_B v_(B_f)^2`.

Solving the two together gives the final velocities, for example

`v_(R_f) = ((m_R - m_B) v_(R_i) + 2 m_B v_(B_i))/(m_R + m_B)`.

Change the masses and the initial velocities and run the simulation; the readout shows each cart's momentum before and after, and the totals. Equal masses exchange their velocities. This simulation is useful for studying conservation of momentum and energy in collisions.
