In this simulation, you can explore the electric field due to point charges. The simulation shows three charges and a point P. A positive charge is indicated with a plus sign (+), if its corresponding slider is set to a positive value, and a negative charge with a minus sign (−), if its slider is set to a negative value. You can drag the charges and the point P around the screen, or use the sliders. The button displays or removes an arrow representing the net electric field at P.

A point charge `q` produces at a distance `r` an electric field of magnitude

`E = k |q|/r^2`,

pointing away from a positive charge and toward a negative one. The field at P is the vector sum of the fields of the three charges:

`vec E_P = vec E_1 + vec E_2 + vec E_3`.

A small positive test charge placed at P would feel a force in the direction of `vec E_P`.
