In this simulation, you can investigate the force that a Magnetic Field exerts on a charged particle.

Activity: Direction of Magnetic Field

Task: Set the magnetic field to a positive value (e.g., B = +2) and observe the direction of the field arrows. Then switch to a negative value (e.g., B = -2) and compare.

Questions:

  1. What happens to the direction of the field arrows when the magnetic field is set to a negative value?
    2. _____
  2. Increasing the magnitude of the magnetic field results in:
    3. _____

Activity: Velocity and Magnetic Force

Task: Set the charge to q = +1 and the magnetic field to B = +2. Vary the velocity components and observe the direction and magnitude of the magnetic force.

Questions:

  1. The magnetic force on a moving charge is:
    2. _____
  2. If the velocity is zero, the magnetic force is:
    3. _____

Activity: Charge Sign and Force Direction

Task: Set the velocity to (vx, vy) = (3, 0) and the magnetic field to B = +2. Observe the force direction for q = +1 and then for q = -1.

Questions:

  1. Reversing the sign of the charge will:
    2. _____
  2. A negative charge moving in a magnetic field experiences a force:
    3. _____
