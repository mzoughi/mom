External Observer View

In the first set up, the simulation shows a side‑view scene of a bridge supported by tall arches... A light brown truck moves left to right on the bridge. A red ball is dropped from the truck when the play button is pressed. The red ball traces a parabolic path (pale red markers) as it falls. A blue ball stays with the truck (pale blue markers in a horizontal line). Horizontal (vx), vertical (vy), and resultant (v) velocity vectors are drawn on the red ball. A timer reads time as the motion progresses. Numerical readouts list positions, velocities, and accelerations. For both the blue ball and the red ball. The readouts for the red ball are in red. The vertical position readout y1, varies from 0 to `-213.44\ "m"`. The vertical velocity v1y, varies from `0` to `-64.68\ "m/s"`, and the vertical acceleration a1y, stays constant at `-9.8\ "m/s"^2`. The horizontal position readout x1, varies from `0` to `105.60\ "m"`. The horizontal velocity v1x, stays constant at `16.00\ "m/s"`, and the vertical acceleration a1x, stays constant at `0\ "m/s"^2`. The readouts for the blue ball are in blue. The horizontal position x2 changes from 0 to `105.60\ "m"`. The horizontal velocity v2x, stays constant at `16.00\ "m/s"`. The horizontal acceleration a2x, stays constant at `0\ "m/s"^2`. A stick figure at the bottom-left represents a stationary outside observer. Next to the "Play" button, two other buttons can be used for slow motion. A fourth button is available to reset the simulation to the initial parameters. A check box is available to visualize the situation relative to someone inside the truck. Select the check box to get a description of that perspective.

Truck's Point of View

In this setup, the scene from the moving truck's frame is shown. The red ball's ghost images (pale red markers) stack vertically under the release point with increasing sepation, indicating straight down speeding motion relative to the truck (no horizontal component). A downward purple vy vector is shown. The blue ball remains at rest on the truck. Numerical readouts appear as before, The vertical position readout y1, varies from 0 to `-490.00\ "m"`. The vertical velocity v1y, varies from `0` to `-9.80\ "m/s"`, and the vertical acceleration a1y, stays constant at `-9.8\ "m/s"^2`. All other quantities for both balls remain at zero. The stick figure and the bridge arches appear to be moving left.
