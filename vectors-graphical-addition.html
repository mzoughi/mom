Dragging Vectors

Directions

Drag any vector by its shaft or arrowhead to move it around the canvas -- its length and direction stay fixed, only its position changes. Arrange the vectors tip-to-tail to add them graphically. The resultant (sum) is always drawn in red from the origin, no matter how you've arranged the vectors, so you can check your graphical addition against it.

Use the measuring tool to read a vector's magnitude and direction: drag the tool's anchor (the dot) onto the vector's tail, then drag its arrowhead out until it matches the vector's tip. The reading below the tool's anchor shows the matched magnitude and angle.
