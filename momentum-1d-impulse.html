This simulation shows a cart that collides and changes its velocity. The change in its momentum is the impulse it receives.

The momentum of a cart of mass `m` moving with velocity `vec v` is `vec p = m vec v`. The impulse is the change in momentum, and it equals the average force multiplied by the time the force acts:

`vec J = Delta vec p = vec p_f - vec p_i = vec F_"avg" Delta t`.

Momentum is a vector: in one dimension its sign gives its direction along `hat"i"`. A cart that bounces back has a change in momentum larger than its initial momentum, because the momentum first goes to zero and then becomes negative.

Change the mass and the initial velocity and run the simulation; the readout shows the initial and final momentum and their difference. This simulation is useful for studying momentum, impulse and the vector nature of momentum.
