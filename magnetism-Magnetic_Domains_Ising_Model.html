What's happening

The lattice is initialized with all spins random (B = 0). Temperature T = 1.00 -- well below the critical temperature of 2.27. At this low temperature, spins tend to align with their neighbors. Press Play to begin the simulation.

2D Ising Model (Metropolis algorithm):
Each spin s = ±1 interacts with its 4 neighbors. Flip probability: if ΔE < 0, always flip; else flip with probability e−ΔE/T.
Critical temperature Tc ≈ 2.27 J/kB. Below Tc: ordered (ferromagnetic). Above: disordered (paramagnetic).
Colors: Green = spin up (+1)   Red = spin down (−1)
