This simulation shows a block launched by a compressed spring up an inclined track with friction. The spring constant, the compression, the mass, the coefficient of friction and the angle of the track can be changed.

While the block is pushed by the spring, its acceleration is

`a = (k x - m g sin theta - mu m g cos theta)/m`.

After it leaves the spring it slows down with `a = -g sin theta - mu g cos theta`. When it stops, it slides back down only if `tan theta > mu`, with `a = -g sin theta + mu g cos theta` (taking up the track as positive).

In terms of energy, the spring's potential energy becomes kinetic energy, gravitational potential energy and the work done against friction, over a distance `d` along the track:

`1/2 k x^2 = 1/2 m v^2 + m g d sin theta + mu m g d cos theta`.

This simulation is useful for studying spring potential energy, friction, inclined planes and the work-energy theorem.
