Rolling Race · Conservation of Energy · Rolling Without Slipping · MOM · 2025

This simulation releases four objects -- a solid sphere, a solid cylinder, a spherical shell and a thin hoop -- at the same moment on an incline, where they roll without slipping. Their mass, radius and the angle of the incline can be changed.

Rolling without slipping links the speed and the rate of turning, `v = omega r`. With a moment of inertia `I = c m r^2`, the acceleration down the incline is

`a = (g sin theta)/(1 + I/(m r^2)) = (g sin theta)/(1 + c)`,

and from energy conservation the speed after dropping a height `h` is `v = sqrt((2 g h)/(1 + c))`. The kinetic energy is shared between translation and rotation, `K_R/K_T = c`.

Mass and radius cancel out: only the shape decides the order. The solid sphere (`c = 2/5`) always wins, then the solid cylinder (`1/2`), the spherical shell (`2/3`), and the thin hoop (`1`) always comes last, because the more of its energy goes into turning, the slower it moves down the incline.

This simulation is useful for studying rolling motion, moment of inertia, and conservation of energy.
