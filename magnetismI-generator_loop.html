Compare DC and AC output on the oscilloscope. Double B or the speed and Upeak doubles. Reverse the field (φ = 180°) or the direction it is being rotated and the DC output changes polarity. Turn B to 90° and watch the commutator switch at the wrong moments.

Flux per turn Φ = B A cos(θ − φ)  |  Faraday: U = −N dΦ/dt = N B A ω sin(θ − φ)  |  Upeak = N B A ω, with N = 573 turns, A = 0.010 m²  |  AC: slip rings pass U straight out  |  DC: the commutator reverses the connections whenever sin θ changes sign -- a one-way output only when B is horizontal (φ = 0° or 180°)  |  Induced current feels F = I L × B, which opposes the motion (Lenz's law)
