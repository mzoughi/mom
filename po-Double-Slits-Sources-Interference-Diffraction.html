This simulation compares two coherent point sources with two slits. Two coherent sources a distance `d` apart make evenly spaced interference fringes of equal brightness,

`I = I_0 cos^2 ((pi d sin theta)/lambda)`,   bright where `d sin theta = m lambda`,

spaced `Delta y ~~ (lambda L)/d` apart on the screen. Real slits have a width `a`, and each one diffracts the light: the same fringes now sit under a single-slit envelope, `((sin beta)/beta)^2` with `beta = (pi a sin theta)/lambda`, and fade away from the centre.

Count the fringes between two marks on the screen, and see how their number changes with the wavelength, the slit separation and the distance to the screen. The face-on view of the screen, with its centimetre scale, makes the counting easier.
