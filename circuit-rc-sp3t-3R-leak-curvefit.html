Lists the points currently plotted, so you can copy them into Capstone, Excel or Sheets.

The SP3T switch has three positions: CHARGE (battery charges C through R1 and R2), OPEN (no current in the circuit; the charge only leaks away slowly through the capacitor’s dielectric) and DISCHARGE (C discharges through R2 and R3). Set any resistor to 0 to replace it with a plain wire. Every reading of VC, VR1, VR2 and VR3 carries random noise, just like a real voltage sensor. Each time you move the switch, a new segment of data begins; pick a single segment under Data to plot it against the time since the switch, t − t0. The linearized plots should be straight lines with slope −1/τ. Their intercepts tell you something too: compare the intercept of ln(VR1/E) with ln(VR2/E). Late in a segment the signal becomes comparable to the noise, so use the fit window to choose which points to fit.
Manual curve fit (available while paused): choose a model, then adjust A, B, C and t0 by hand until the curve matches the points in the fit window; the smaller the RMS residual, the better the fit.
