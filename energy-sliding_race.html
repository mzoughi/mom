Frictionless Sliding Race · Faraday's Law · MOM · 2025

This simulation releases four objects -- a solid sphere, a solid cylinder, a spherical shell and a thin hoop -- at the same moment on a frictionless incline. Their mass, radius and the angle of the incline can be changed.

Without friction, nothing exerts a torque about an object's center, so none of them starts to turn: `omega = 0` and there is no rotational kinetic energy. Each object simply slides, and Newton's second law along the incline gives the same acceleration for all of them:

`a = g sin theta`.

The moment of inertia, `I = c m r^2`, plays no part. All four objects arrive together, with the same speed `v = sqrt(2 g h)`: the race is always a tie. The lesson is why: compare with the rolling race, where friction makes the objects turn.

This simulation is useful for studying motion on an incline, conservation of energy, and the role of friction in rolling.
