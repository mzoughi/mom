Force Table

Directions

A ring surrounds the pin at the center. Each force's string is tied to the ring -- drag along the ring to change a force's angle, or drag its arrowhead outward/inward to change its magnitude. You can also select a force below and use its Angle and Magnitude sliders.

When all the forces sum to zero, the ring floats centered on the pin. Otherwise the unbalanced net force drags the ring off-center until it presses against the pin -- the more unbalanced, the harder it presses. Adjust the forces until the ring settles back on the pin.
