What's happening

28 ferromagnetic domains are shown with random initial orientations. No external field is applied (B = 0 T). Each domain has a different coercive field -- the minimum field needed to flip it. Apply a field using the slider or preset buttons to begin alignment.

Domains align with applied field -- stronger B causes more uniform alignment.
Each domain has a unique coercive field threshold (hysteresis behavior).
