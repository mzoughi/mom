Part 1: Magnets:

  1. In the simulation, what happens when a single magnet is flipped from horizontal to horizontal‑flipped?
  2. When placing two magnets side‑by‑side and flipping only the left magnet, what change is most noticeable in the field pattern?
  3. With two magnets facing each other north‑to‑north, what happens when one magnet is flipped so its south pole faces the other magnet?
  4. When three magnets are arranged in a row, how does flipping the middle magnet affect the field along the line of magnets?
  5. After breaking a magnet into two pieces, what property do the two pieces immediately gain?
  6. When one magnet is vertical and another horizontal, what best describes the resulting field?
  7. Two broken magnets are brought close together. Which configuration produces the strongest field gradient?
  8. When you rotate a single magnet through all four orientations, which orientation produces the strongest field directly above the magnet?
  9. In a triangular arrangement of three magnets, how does flipping only one magnet affect the central field region?
  10. When two broken pieces of a magnet are moved close together again, what happens to the combined field pattern?
