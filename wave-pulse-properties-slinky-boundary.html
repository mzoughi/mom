This simulation sends a single pulse along two slinkies joined end to end. The pulse is longitudinal: the coils are pushed together (a compression) or pulled apart, along the direction the pulse travels. The spring constants and masses of the slinkies can be changed.

The speed of a pulse on a slinky depends on its stiffness and its mass. For a slinky of length `L`, spring constant `K` and mass `M`,

`v = L sqrt(K/M)`.

At the joint, part of the pulse is reflected and part is transmitted into the second slinky, just as for two ropes. Going into a slower slinky, the reflected pulse comes back inverted (a push returns as a pull).

The tracked coils and the caliper help measure how far and how fast the pulse travels in each slinky.
