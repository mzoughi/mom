Work & Electrical Potential Energy

  
Final distance rf (5-15 cm): 10 cm
Position of q (from ∞ down to rf): ∞

This simulation shows the work needed to bring a small charge `q` from very far away (infinity) to a final distance `r_f` from a large fixed charge `Q`. The signs of both charges and the final distance can be chosen; the position slider moves `q` in from infinity.

The electric potential energy of the pair at a distance `r` is

`U = k (Q q)/r`,   with `U = 0` at infinity.

Bringing `q` in slowly, the applied force does work `W_"app"` while the electric force does work `W_E`. The kinetic energy does not change, so

`W_"app" = Delta U = k (Q q)/r_f`,   `W_E = -Delta U`.

For like charges, positive work must be done to push them together and the potential energy rises; for opposite charges, the electric force does the work and the potential energy falls. The bar chart shows `W_"app"`, `W_E` and `Delta U` as `q` moves in.

This simulation is useful for studying electric potential energy, the work done by and against the electric force, and the choice of zero at infinity.
