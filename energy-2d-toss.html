This simulation shows an object tossed in two dimensions and moving in free fall, with no air resistance. The initial velocity can be set by its components `(v_x, v_y)` or by its magnitude and direction `(|vec v|, theta)`.

Only gravity acts, so the horizontal velocity stays constant and the vertical velocity changes at the rate `g`:

`v_x = v_(ix)`,   `v_y = v_(iy) - g t`,   `x = v_(ix) t`,   `y = y_0 + v_(iy) t - 1/2 g t^2`.

The components and the polar form are related by `v_x = |vec v| cos theta` and `v_y = |vec v| sin theta`. The momentum is `vec p = m vec v`: its horizontal component stays constant, while its vertical component changes because of the force of gravity.

The mechanical energy is conserved:

`K + U_g = 1/2 m v^2 + m g y = "constant"`.

This simulation is useful for studying projectile motion, vector components, momentum and conservation of energy.
